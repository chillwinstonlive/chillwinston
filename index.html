Chill Winston Poster

A personal publishing tool for the Chill Winston gaming channel.

Chill Winston Poster is a private tool used only by the owner of the Chill Winston channels (YouTube @chillwinston, TikTok @chillwinstonlive, Instagram). It runs on the owner's own computer and helps publish short gameplay clips: it writes titles and captions, asks the owner to approve each post, uploads the approved clip to the owner's own accounts, and reads those posts' view counts so future posts can improve.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
