Privacy Policy

Chill Winston Poster · Last updated 15 November 2026

Who this covers

Chill Winston Poster is a private tool used only by the owner of the Chill Winston channels to publish their own videos. It has no other users and collects no data from members of the public.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (through the YouTube API Services): uploading videos to the owner's channel, reading public information about recent YouTube Shorts for title research, and reading view, like, comment and retention statistics for the owner's own videos.
  • TikTok and Instagram (when connected): sending the owner's videos to their own accounts and reading statistics for the owner's own posts.

How data is stored and used

  • Everything is stored only on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in Windows Credential Manager; post details and statistics are kept in a local database.
  • Video analysis (speech-to-text and image description) runs locally on that computer.
  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to improve future titles, captions and posting times.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok and Instagram data is handled under their own privacy policies.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google account permissions and from the app settings in TikTok and Instagram. Deleting the tool's folder from the owner's computer deletes all stored data.
